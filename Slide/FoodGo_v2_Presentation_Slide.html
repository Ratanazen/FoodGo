SLIDE 02 • PROBLEM & SOLUTION

Designed for Cambodia's Digital Food Ecosystem

  • Bilingual Khmer & English UI: 1-tap toggle between English and ភាសាខ្មែរ with Noto Sans Khmer typography.
  • Dual Currency Engine: Real-time USD ($) and KHR (៛) display (1 USD = 4,100 KHR) with automatic free delivery on orders >= $15.00.
  • Bakong / ABA KHQR Integration: Supports ABA PAY, Bakong, ACLEDA, and Wing with EMVCo QR payload copy and timer refresh.
  • Configurable API Hosting: Switch between 127.0.0.1:8000, Android Emulator 10.0.2.2:8000, or LAN/Cloud server with live /api/health/ testing.
