# FoodGo v2.0 Showcase Video — Storyboard & Production Script

**Duration**: 5 Minutes 30 Seconds  
**Resolution**: 1920×1080 (Full HD, 16:9, 30 FPS)  
**Narration Language**: Khmer (ភាសាខ្មែរ) with English Subtitles  
**Audio Track**: Modern uplifting ambient electronic with traditional Khmer Roneat acoustic accents  

---

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: Introduction & Brand Evolution (0:00 – 0:45)
- **Visual**: Cinematic zoom into Phnom Penh skyline transitioning to FoodGo v2.0 animated splash screen.
- **On Screen Text**: "FoodGo v2.0 — Redefining Cambodian Food Delivery".
- **Action**: Demonstrates instant non-blocking startup and Khmer / English localization toggle.

### Scene 2: Culinary Discovery & Authentic Dishes (0:45 – 1:45)
- **Visual**: Scrolling through the redesigned Home screen showing Cambodian gastronomy categories: *Khmer Classics (Fish Amok, Beef Lok Lak), Noodle Soups (Phnom Penh Kuy Teav), Street BBQ (Bai Sach Chrouk)*.
- **Action**: Tapping a dish to open the interactive **Food Customizer Modal** with portion size, spice levels, and paid add-ons.

### Scene 3: Cart, Free Delivery & Instant KHQR Payment (1:45 – 2:45)
- **Visual**: Cart screen showcasing the Phnom Penh free delivery meter, driver tip selector, and instant checkout.
- **Action**: Opening the **GlassQrPayModal**, toggling between USD and KHR (`៛`), scanning the authentic Bakong QR code, and payment verification.

### Scene 4: Live Order Tracking (2:45 – 3:30)
- **Visual**: OpenStreetMap view with animated driver movement, preparation timeline, and in-app call/chat actions.

### Scene 5: Driver Delivery Portal (3:30 – 4:15)
- **Visual**: Switching to Driver Mode (`/driver`), toggling Online duty, accepting a 30s radar dispatch, and progressing through trip milestones.

### Scene 6: Restaurant Kitchen KDS Portal (4:15 – 4:55)
- **Visual**: Restaurant Manager screen (`/admin`) displaying the live orders Kanban board and instant menu inventory toggle.

### Scene 7: Technical Architecture, Testing & Conclusion (4:55 – 5:30)
- **Visual**: Architecture diagram showing Flutter, Django REST Framework, and NBC Bakong KHQR, followed by automated test execution results (100% pass rate).
- **Closing**: "FoodGo v2.0 — Available on iOS, Android & Web".
